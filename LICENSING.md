# Smart2Raw Licensing (open-core)

Smart2Raw follows an **open-core** model, with two editions.

## Open edition — this repository

Licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later**
(`AGPL-3.0-or-later`). You may freely use, study, modify and redistribute it,
subject to the AGPL.

AGPL points to note (informal summary — the license text prevails):
- Derivative works and modified versions must stay under the same license.
- **Network clause:** if you run a modified version to provide a service over a
  network, you must make the corresponding source available to its users.
- Because it is *header-only*, embedding Smart2Raw normally makes the host
  program a derivative work for AGPL purposes.

### Commercial license for the open edition
For those who **cannot/do not want** to comply with the AGPL (proprietary use or
a closed network service), the open edition is also available under a
**commercial license** that waives the copyleft obligations.

## Advanced edition — not public

There is an **advanced edition** of Smart2Raw, with additional features, that is
**not published** as open source. It is offered **exclusively under a commercial
license**. See `EDITIONS.md` for the feature boundary.
