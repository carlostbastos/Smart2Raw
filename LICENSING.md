# Licensing (open-core)

Smart2Raw follows an **open-core** model with two editions.

## Open edition — this repository

Licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later**
(`AGPL-3.0-or-later`). You may use, study, modify and redistribute it freely,
provided you comply with the AGPL.

AGPL highlights (informal summary — the license text prevails):
- Derivative and modified works must be made available under the same license.
- **Network clause:** if you run a modified version to offer a service over a
  network, you must make the corresponding source available to its users.
- Being header-only, embedding Smart2Raw usually makes the host program a
  derivative work for AGPL purposes.

### Commercial license for the open edition
For those who **cannot or do not wish** to comply with the AGPL (proprietary use
or a closed network service), the open edition is also available under a
**commercial license** that waives the copyleft obligations.

## Advanced edition — not public

There is an **advanced edition** of Smart2Raw, with additional capabilities, that
is **not** released as open source. It is available **exclusively under a
commercial license**. See `EDITIONS.md`.
